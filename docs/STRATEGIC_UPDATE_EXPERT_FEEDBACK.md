### Why UPSC is Strongest

| Metric | UPSC | JEE | Finance |
|--------|------|-----|---------|
| Discussion-heavy | ✅ Very | ❌ Prefer direct answers | ⚠️ Can be noisy |
| Community-driven | ✅ Yes | ❌ Coaching-dependent | ⚠️ Money-focused |
| Essay format fits | ✅ Perfect | ❌ Mismatch | ❌ Wrong format |

| Moderation difficulty | ✅ Moderate | ✅ Moderate | ❌ Very hard |
| Misinformation risk | ✅ Low | ✅ Low | ❌ Very high |

### Why Not JEE

JEE students:
- Prefer direct answers (not discussion)
- Use established coaching ecosystems
- Churn immediately after exam
- Create toxic competition dynamics
- Don't form learning communities

### Why Not Finance

Finance communities:
- Become speculative very quickly
- Require heavy moderation
- High misinformation risk
- Users optimize for tips, not learning
- Wrong community DNA

### The Strategy

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Build MVP for UPSC only**
- All 3 features, but UPSC category only
- Seed with 1000+ UPSC content
- Launch to UPSC community (Reddit, WhatsApp groups)
- Get 500+ active UPSC users

**Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Add JEE**
- Expand feature set if needed
- Learning from UPSC community
- Different UX/mechanics for JEE if needed

**Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): Add Finance**
- Only if UPSC + JEE mature
- May need different moderation

### Why This Matters

All activity concentrated in one domain = interaction density = community vibrancy

Spread across 3 domains = ghosttown = no return visits

## PART 6: DOWNVOTES ARE DANGEROUS (Strategic Change)

### Current Plan: Hybrid Rating (Upvotes + Downvotes)

### Expert Recommendation: Reconsider

In student communities, public downvotes cause:
- ❌ Discourages beginners
- ❌ Creates toxicity
- ❌ Reduces contributions
- ❌ Fear of sharing

### Better Alternative

Use positive descriptors:
- "Helpful" ✅
- "Well explained" ✅
- "Needs correction" ⚠️
- "Incomplete" ⚠️

Instead of "Downvote" ❌

### Behind the Scenes

You can INTERNALLY:
- Track who marks "Needs correction"
- Penalize misinformation
- Adjust reputation scores
- Flag problematic content

### But Socially

Keep environment positive.
Educational communities thrive on:
- Support
- Constructive feedback
- Psychological safety
- Not public shaming

### Updated System

```
Public Display:
├── Upvotes
├── "Helpful" marks
├── "Well explained" marks
└── Comments with feedback

Internal Reputation:
├── Counts "Needs correction"
├── Tracks "Incomplete"
├── AI verdict (CORRECT/INCORRECT)
├── Penalizes misinformation
└── Adjusts creator score

User sees:
├── Recognition (positive)
└── Growth path (how to improve)

User doesn't see:
├── Public downvotes
├── Shame signals
└── Negative reputation
```

This is crucial for retention.
