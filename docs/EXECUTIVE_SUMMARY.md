# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Peer Learning Platform - Complete Design Session Output

---

## THE IDEA IN ONE SENTENCE

A free, community-driven platform where students solve daily exam questions, get peer feedback, and learn through verified peer-to-peer knowledge sharing, organized by curriculum.

---

## THE PROBLEM

Students waste 3+ hours finding answers that should take 5 minutes because current solutions are:
- Chaotic (YouTube: 1.2M+ results per topic)
- Algorithm-driven (no authentic community)
- Profit-focused (Doubtnut, Vedantu behind paywalls)
- Scattered (WhatsApp groups, lost information)
- Ephemeral (Claude: answers disappear)

---

## THE SOLUTION

**5 Pillars:**

1. **Daily Questions** - 1 question/day drives daily habit + competition
2. **Peer Feedback** - Multiple perspectives, community-verified  
3. **AI Verification** - 5-layer validation ensures quality
4. **Study Groups** - Accountability + community bonding
5. **Free Forever** - Mission-driven, not profit-driven

---

## KEY INNOVATIONS

| Innovation | Benefit | Why Unique |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Daily Questions** | 8x engagement | Nobody else does this for exam prep |
| **4-Level Hierarchy** | Organized like textbook | Not algorithm chaos |
| **Hybrid Rating** | Quality control | Up/downvotes + AI + community |

## CRITICAL DESIGN DECISIONS

✅ **4 Levels** (not 3) - Match UPSC official structure
✅ **YouTube Embedding** (not file upload) - Cost-effective, user control
✅ **Hybrid Rating** (up/downvotes) - Quality control without toxicity
✅ **AI in Phase 1** (not Phase 3) - Build trust immediately
✅ **Group Verification** - 5 uploads + 20 upvotes + 75% accuracy minimum
